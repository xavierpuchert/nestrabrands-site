Nestra Brands is a consumer brand company that builds original products and the brands around them. It owns the design, the firmware, the app and the name, and works with manufacturing partners to build at volume. Lumi is the first.

Founded by Xavier Puchert and Petr Vinograd, registered in Estonia in September 2026, and run from wherever the work is.
